Intelligent Architecture

Built for Complete Control & Convenience

From optical receipt recognition to bank SMS regex processing—manage money smarter.

Agentic AI Chat (BYOK Gemini API)

Interact with your financial assistant using simple natural language text or voice commands. Snap receipt photos directly inside the app, and Gemini AI parses store names, invoice numbers, timestamps, and total amounts directly into your ledger.

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) - Zero middleman servers or fees.
  • Multimodal Receipt OCR for paper bills and digital invoices.

Automatic Bank SMS Sync

Automate debits and credits from bank notification SMS (e.g. StanChart, bKash, and local banks). The app reads local bank texts, matches approved Merchant Whitelists, and logs exact balances in real-time.

  • Merchant Whitelist ensures unwanted personal SMS are ignored.
  • Failed sync queue for easy manual approval.

Reports, Charts & Quick Manual Entry

Prefer manual logging? Use the instant transaction drawer with custom tags (Grocery, Fruits, CashIn) and accounts (bKash, Cash, StanChart). View comprehensive charts and running balance feeds anytime.

Zero Cloud Lock-in

Your Data Stays strictly On Your Phone

Expense Tracker Agent is architected with a local-first policy. All transaction feeds, categories, running balances, and SMS sync rules reside in your phone's native SQLite database.
